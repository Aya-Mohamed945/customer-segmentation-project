\## 2. Overall Recommendations



\### 2.1 Loyalty Program Redesign

\- \*\*Tiered System Based on Segments:\*\*

&#x20; - 🏆 Platinum (High-Income Professionals): Premium benefits.

&#x20; - ⭐ Gold (Young Professionals): Digital rewards.

&#x20; - 🔵 Silver (Mid-Range/Seniors): Value rewards.

&#x20; - 🟢 Bronze (Entry-Level): Student discounts.



\### 2.2 Marketing Channel Optimization

| Segment | Primary Channel | Secondary Channel |

|---------|-----------------|-------------------|

| High-Income Professionals | Email/Lifestyle | In-store events |

| Young Professionals | Instagram/Mobile | Influencers |

| Seniors | Direct Mail | Radio/TV |

| Executives | LinkedIn/Direct | Corporate |

| Entry-Level | TikTok/Campus | Referrals |



\### 2.3 Product Strategy Recommendations

\- \*\*Premium (High-Income):\*\* Expand organic, gourmet items.

\- \*\*Convenience (Young Professionals):\*\* Meal kits, quick meals.

\- \*\*Value (Entry-Level):\*\* Budget bundles, student discounts.

\- \*\*Comfort (Seniors):\*\* Easy-open packaging.
